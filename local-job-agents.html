02

Three tiers

The tier tells you more than the feature list does.

Presses Submit

Discovery to sent application, unattended. One bad config away from sending something you never read.

  • AIHawk
  • ApplyPilot
  • scout
  • agentic-job-applier
  • claude-job-auto-apply

Fills, never sends

The form is completed for you. You fix the one wrong answer and click. Slower, far safer.

  • JobNavigator
  • MR.Jobs
  • AutoApply

Decides

The half nobody automates: is the posting real, is it still open, is it worth your evening.

  • career-ops
  • ApplyPack
  • JobSpy
03

The India catch

Every auto-submit path is built for Western hiring.

The ATS lists are all Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS. Indian employers hire through Naukri and LinkedIn — and most of these tools skip LinkedIn Easy Apply because it bans accounts fastest.

JobSpy is the only piece with native Naukri support, and it does not apply. Expect the submit half to earn its keep on multinationals hiring into India; the rest of the work is discovery and tailoring.

04

How to choose

Want it to actually submit

AIHawk or ApplyPilot

AIHawk for the community, ApplyPilot for the cleaner stages and real dry-run.

Want a console, not a black box

JobNavigator or MR.Jobs

Both Docker, both autofill rather than submit. MR.Jobs has the safer defaults.

Want better decisions, not more applications

career-ops

Scores the JD, checks the posting, drafts the documents. You click Submit.

Want to spend nothing

scout, ApplyPack or career-ops

All three run on a free Gemini tier, Ollama, or the CLI subscription you already have.
